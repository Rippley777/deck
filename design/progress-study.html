Evaluation: silhouette at 16 px · completion without color · room for status · visual weight in navigation.
Selected: Segmented ring. It keeps its silhouette at 16 px and leaves room for hollow / filled cards, notches, and a quiet center.
Layered arcs crowd the center. Card fans lose individual cards at small sizes. Stack meters read cleanly but feel more like a meter than a deck.
The final component uses hollow annular cards, with gaps retained even when all work is complete.
